## Figure 2

Caption (author's final wording, manuscript V9):

Figure 2. Capacity-threshold screening sensitivity for 2035_EB future_5: (a) selected upgrade tier
across the configured demand range; (b) detail of the 139.500 MW threshold crossing.

Values shown (verified by S20 from the screening code): effective headroom 42.5 MW (50 MW synthetic
base × U_headroom_mult 0.85); tier thresholds 42.5, 63.5, 74.5, 139.5 and 192.5 MW; Path A
(declared peak) 132.998 MW → +97 MW; Path B (peak × U_inc_mult 1.05) 139.648 MW → +150 MW.
Selection depends on capacity only; the configured base costs (NZ$55.9 million and NZ$75.0 million)
are not plotted and do not enter it. Modelled screening outcome under configured synthetic assumptions.

How the figure is computed:

- Declared peak: canonical IEP artefact `2035_EB` `future_5`.
- `U_headroom_mult` (0.85) and `U_inc_mult` (1.05): `rdm/futures.csv`, future_id 5.
- Tiers: `grid_upgrades_southland_edendale.toml`, read with the unmodified `load_upgrade_menu()`.
- Selection: the retained wrapper `select_adequate_upgrade()`, which calls the unmodified
  `gxp_rdm_screen.evaluate_upgrade_option()` and selects the smallest tier with zero exceedance
  (tolerance 10⁻⁶ MW).
- The plotted step function is built from headroom plus added capacity, and each step is checked
  against the selection function on both sides of its threshold before plotting.
- Above 192.5 MW no menu tier is adequate, so no step is drawn there.
- Costs are not plotted, because they do not affect selection. The future-specific capex multiplier
  (`U_upgrade_capex_mult` = 1.15) affects only the annualised cost computed by the screen, not the
  selected tier; the manuscript reports the configured base costs.

The 21, 32 and 97 MW tiers and their costs reproduce EECA (2022), RETA Southland Phase One Report,
p. 74. The 150 MW tier and its NZ$75.0 million cost are an illustrative screening assumption whose
derivation is not documented; they are not an official estimate.

`expected/paper_reported_screening_values.json` holds the manuscript's values, which
`scripts/verify.py` (S12) and `../scripts/verify_screening_tier_mapping.py` (S20) check.
