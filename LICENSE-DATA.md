## Excluded third-party material

Values reproduced from Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority (EECA) (2022), *Regional Energy
Transition Accelerator (RETA): Southland – Phase One Report*, are **not** licensed by this repository.
They are included with attribution so that the reported calculations can be reproduced, and they
remain subject to their source's terms. They are:

- the 21, 32 and 97 MW connection options, with their security levels and capital costs;
- the biomass resource totals and categories;
- the moisture content and net calorific value;
- the delivered-cost tiers and the transport-distance basis.

`DATA_SOURCES.md` gives the source pages. `EECA_CONTENT_INVENTORY.csv` lists every file that states,
restates or derives from these values. Where such values appear inside a file that is otherwise
licensed, this licence covers the rest of that file only. The source report itself is not included.
